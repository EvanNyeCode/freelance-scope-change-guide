
# Freelance quote template

Use this copy-ready structure to make a project price easy for a client to review before work begins. Replace the brackets with the actual project terms, remove anything that does not apply, and confirm the final agreement in writing. This is planning guidance, not legal, tax, accounting, or financial advice.

> AI assistance disclosure: this template was prepared with AI assistance and reviewed for practical planning use. Adapt it to the actual project and agreement.

## Quote summary

- **Client:** [name or organization]
- **Project:** [short project name]
- **Prepared on:** [date]
- **Valid through:** [date, if you use an expiry]
- **Outcome:** [the result the client is hiring you to achieve]
- **Total investment:** [amount and currency]

## What is included

### Deliverables

- [deliverable 1, with a concrete completion description]
- [deliverable 2]
- [deliverable 3]

### Assumptions and client inputs

- [content, access, approvals, decisions, or files the client supplies]
- [technical, platform, or third-party assumptions]
- [response time or review-window assumption]

### Timeline and milestones

- **Start:** [date or trigger]
- **Milestone 1:** [deliverable and target date]
- **Milestone 2:** [deliverable and target date]
- **Final delivery:** [date or condition]

Dates depend on the stated client inputs and approval timing. If those inputs or approvals move, the delivery plan may need to move too.

### Revisions and acceptance

- Included revisions: [number or clear description]
- Feedback format: [one consolidated list, review call, or other method]
- Acceptance check: [what counts as complete]
- Defect correction: [how issues against the agreed acceptance criteria are handled]

## What is not included

- [excluded deliverable, platform, integration, or ongoing service]
- [additional revision rounds or new direction]
- [content, licenses, hosting, or third-party charges not supplied by you]

If a request changes the deliverables, assumptions, revision allowance, acceptance criteria, or deadline, pause and price it as a separate change before starting the added work.

## Price and payment

- **Project price:** [amount]
- **Payment schedule:** [deposit or milestone amounts and due dates]
- **Payment method:** [method]
- **Taxes or third-party charges:** [who is responsible, if applicable]

The price covers the included work above. It is not a promise that undefined future requests are included.

## Change process

> A request outside this quote will be described in writing with its added price and delivery impact. The client can approve the change or keep the original scope and schedule. Added work begins only after written approval.

## Approval

Please reply with written confirmation that you approve the deliverables, assumptions, timeline, revisions, exclusions, price, payment schedule, and change process above.

- **Client approval:** [name]
- **Approval date:** [date]
- **Notes:** [optional project-specific note]

## Final review before sending

- Is the outcome concrete enough that both sides can recognize it?
- Are deliverables, assumptions, client inputs, revisions, exclusions, and acceptance criteria visible?
- Is there one clear price and a payment schedule?
- Are the timeline dependencies and change process stated before work begins?
- Can the client approve the exact version being quoted?

For a separate scope-change response, use the free QuoteBoundary workflow described in the main guide. Keep project details in the appropriate private workflow and adapt the final wording to the actual agreement.
