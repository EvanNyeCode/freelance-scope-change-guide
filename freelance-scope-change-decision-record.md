# Freelance scope-change decision record

Use this short record when a client request may change the agreed deliverable, revision limit, client input, acceptance criteria, or deadline. It keeps the original scope visible while making the new tradeoff easy to approve.

This is planning guidance, not a contract or legal advice.

## The five-minute record

**Project:**  
**Client / stakeholder:**  
**Date:**  

### 1. Approved baseline

- **Outcome:** What was approved?
- **Deliverables:** What is included?
- **Assumptions and client inputs:** What must remain true?
- **Revisions and acceptance:** What is the agreed review and acceptance process?
- **Schedule and price:** What date and investment were approved?

### 2. Requested change

- **Request in plain language:**  
- **What it changes:** deliverable / revision round / input / acceptance criterion / deadline / other
- **Why it is outside the baseline:**  
- **What remains unchanged:**  

### 3. Impact

Estimate the addition before agreeing to it.

- **Added work:**  
- **Added hours or effort:**  
- **Rate or pricing basis:**  
- **Added price:**  
- **Schedule or deadline impact:**  
- **New assumptions, risks, or dependencies:**  

Keep real expenses, coordination, uncertainty, or rush adjustments separate and explain them rather than hiding them inside a buffer.

### 4. Choice

Offer a clear choice instead of silently absorbing the request.

**Option A — keep the approved scope**  
Deliver the original outcome, price, and schedule. Treat the request as a later phase or separate project.

**Option B — approve the change**  
Add the described work for **[added price]**, with delivery moving to **[revised date]** and the assumptions above.

### 5. Written decision

- **Decision:** keep baseline / approve change
- **Approved price and timing:**  
- **Approver:**  
- **Approval date:**  
- **Reference or attachment:**  

Do not start the added work until the changed work, price, and schedule have written approval under the project’s agreement.

## Copy-ready client message

> Thanks for the request. **[Change]** is outside the current **[deliverable, revision limit, or timeline]**. I can add it for **[price]**, with delivery moving to **[date]**, or we can keep the approved scope and schedule this as a follow-on phase. Please confirm which option you prefer before I begin the added work.
