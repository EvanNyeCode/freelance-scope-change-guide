# Freelance Change Request Template

Use this copy-ready planning template when a client asks for work that may change the agreed deliverables, revision limit, price, or timeline. It is communication guidance, not a contract or legal advice.

## Change request

- **Project:** [project name]
- **Date:** [date]
- **Requested change:** [describe the new or changed work]
- **Reason or context:** [what prompted the request]

## Scope impact

- **Current agreement:** [the deliverable, revision limit, input, acceptance criterion, or deadline already agreed]
- **What is different:** [the new output, extra round, new input, or timing change]
- **Added effort or coordination:** [hours, dependencies, or risk to account for]

## Price and timing

- **Added price:** [price]
- **Schedule impact:** [new delivery date or added business days]
- **Alternative:** [keep the approved scope and schedule this as a follow-on phase]

## Approval

Please confirm whether you approve the changed scope for **[price]** and the updated timing of **[date]** before the additional work begins. Until written approval is received, the previously approved scope and schedule remain unchanged.

## Short client message

> Thanks for the request to [describe the change]. It sits outside the current agreement because it adds [deliverable, revisions, timing, or other work]. I can add it for [price], with delivery moving to [date], or we can keep the approved scope and schedule this as a follow-on phase. Please confirm which option you prefer before I begin the added work.

For the context, examples, and five-step checklist, return to the [freelance scope-change checklist](README.md).
