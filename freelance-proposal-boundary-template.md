# Freelance proposal boundary template

Use this copy-ready structure to make a fixed-price freelance proposal explicit before work begins. It keeps the deliverables, assumptions, revisions, payment, and change process visible so a client can approve a real tradeoff.

This is planning language, not a contract or legal advice. Adapt it to the agreement and jurisdiction that apply to the project.

## Revisions and exclusions

- Included revision rounds: [number]
- A revision is: [definition]
- Not included: [out-of-scope work, third-party costs, or additional deliverables]

## Investment and payment

- Project investment: [price and currency]
- Payment schedule: [deposit / milestones / final payment]
- Payment method and due dates: [terms]

## Changes after approval

Requests that add deliverables, revision rounds, client inputs, stakeholders, or urgency are reviewed before the added work begins. The change response will state the added price, schedule impact, and approval step. The original scope and schedule remain the alternative until the change is approved in writing.
