# Freelance Scope-Change Email Template

Use this copy-ready email when a client request changes the agreed deliverables, revision limit, inputs, or delivery timing. It is communication guidance, not a contract or legal advice.

## Subject

Change to [project] — updated scope, price, and timing

## Email

Hi [client name],

Thanks for the request to [describe the change]. It sits outside the current agreement because it adds [deliverable, revisions, timing, or other work].

I can add it for [price], with delivery moving to [date]. Alternatively, we can keep the approved scope and schedule this as a follow-on phase.

Please confirm which option you prefer before I begin the additional work. Until then, the previously approved scope and schedule remain unchanged.

Best,

[your name]

## Before sending

- Name the changed work in plain language.
- State the added price and schedule impact.
- Keep the original scope available as an alternative.
- Wait for written approval before starting the added work.

For the five-step checklist and planning context, return to the [freelance scope-change guide](README.md).
