# Freelance scope-change pricing worksheet

Use this worksheet before replying to a client who asks for added deliverables, extra revisions, a new integration, or a faster deadline. It helps you price the change from the work and delivery impact instead of guessing from the original project total.

> AI assistance disclosure: this worksheet was prepared with AI assistance and reviewed for practical planning use. Adapt it to the actual project and agreement. It is not legal, tax, accounting, or financial advice.

## 1. Re-state the original baseline

- **Original outcome:** [what the project was meant to deliver]
- **Original deliverables:** [the agreed list]
- **Original deadline:** [date or milestone]
- **Original revision allowance:** [number or description]
- **Original price and payment status:** [amount and paid/unpaid]

If the request fits the original baseline, treat it as clarification or included work. If it changes the baseline, continue the worksheet before promising a date.

## 2. Describe the requested change

- **Requested addition:** [plain-language description]
- **Why it is outside the baseline:** [new output, new dependency, new revision, or new timing]
- **Client choice needed:** [approve the change, keep the original scope, or remove another item]

## 3. Estimate the incremental impact

| Impact | Estimate | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Added production hours | [hours] | [design, writing, development, testing, or setup] |
| Added coordination hours | [hours] | [meetings, approvals, vendor/client dependencies] |
| Rush or schedule impact | [amount or days] | [only when the timing change is real] |
| Uncertainty buffer | [hours or amount] | [only for a concrete unknown] |
| **Total added effort** | **[total]** | [show the arithmetic] |

Price the added effort with the same rate or pricing logic used for the original project unless the change creates a clearly different risk, dependency, or deadline.

**Added effort price:** `[total added effort] Ã— [rate or agreed unit price] = [amount]`

Add a separate, clearly explained rush or coordination amount only when it represents real additional work or delivery impact. Do not hide the change inside the original price.

## 4. Give the client a clean decision

Offer one clear added price and timing, then state the alternative:

> I can add **[requested change]** for **[added price]**. It would move delivery to **[new date or impact]**. If you would rather keep the current **[original price/date]**, I will continue with the original scope and leave this item out. Please confirm which option you want before I start the added work.

Keep the request, price, delivery impact, and approval in the same written thread. Do not begin the added work on an implied approval.

## Final check

- Does the change clearly differ from the original baseline?
- Is the added effort visible and defensible?
- Is the added price stated once, without a hidden range?
- Is any rush or coordination impact real and explained?
- Did the client choose the added work or the original scope in writing?
- Did you receive written approval before starting the added work?
- Did you update the delivery plan after approval?
